None helps: splitting 162 claims leaves too few per group to tune extra parameters, so they overfit. The patterns are
real but describe *where* the system is weak rather than giving a cheap fix. The actionable directions they point to are
(1) selecting a second abstract for multi-document claims and (2) stronger evidence detection for claims with no
training twin, i.e. better models rather than better thresholds.
